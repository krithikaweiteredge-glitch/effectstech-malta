Prologue

There are weddings, and there are weddings people talk about for a generation. We photograph and film the second kind on an island of honey-coloured stone, baroque palaces and a sea that changes colour by the hour.

What follows is not a portfolio. It is how your celebration in Malta could look, chapter by chapter, through the eyes of the people who will be standing quietly beside you.

Chapter I

The Arrival

Valletta · Mdina

Your families fly in over the Grand Harbour.

The first frames are of the island itself: a capital built by knights, the Silent City on its hill, long lunches under fortress walls while everyone falls a little in love with Malta.

Chapter II

Mehndi on the Bastions

Saluting Battery · Valletta

Henna, marigold and the sea breeze.

Low lounges set between centuries-old cannons, the harbour turning gold behind you. We photograph the details your mothers chose, the laughter you won't remember, and the light that makes it all look like a painting.

Chapter III

The Sangeet

The Vaulted Halls · Valletta

A night that refuses to end.

Stone vaults lit like a cathedral and dressed like a ballroom. Live orchestra, your family on the stage, a dance floor that doesn't empty until dawn. We work fast and unseen, so every frame feels like being there again.

Chapter IV

Vows by the Sea

St Julian's

White flowers, open sky, and the Mediterranean behind you.

Your pheras on a seafront terrace, timed to the minute so the sun sets as you take the last round. Then moonrise, and the mandap glowing over the water.

We time the last phera to the sunset. To the minute.

Chapter V

Wonderland

Palazzo Parisio · Naxxar

A baroque palace, a masked gala.

Gilded salons, performers who don't blink, a banquet that looks like a Renaissance painting. This is where our cinematographers do their most extravagant work.

Chapter VI

The Farewell

Grand Harbour · Valletta

The cannons fire across the harbour.

A private salute from the Saluting Battery, the city lit gold, and one last portrait of the two of you before the island lets you go. Some endings deserve to be heard across a city, and photographed properly.
