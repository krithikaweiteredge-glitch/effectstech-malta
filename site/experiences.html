Questions

Before you ask

How far in advance should we enquire?

Malta weddings cluster in late spring and early autumn, and we take a small number each season. Most couples reach us twelve to eighteen months ahead; shorter timelines are often possible, so do ask.

Does your team travel to Malta for the wedding?

Yes. Our photographers, cinematographers and producer arrive before your first function so we can walk every venue in the light we will be shooting in.

Can you fly drones over Valletta and the Grand Harbour?

Drone coverage is subject to Maltese aviation rules and venue permissions. We handle the applications and only promise aerial shots where they are permitted.

When do we receive our photographs and films?

A teaser within two weeks, your full gallery within six to eight weeks, and feature films and albums shortly after. Platinum couples see a same-day edit at their reception.

Do you cover Gozo and other venues outside Valletta?

Of course. Mdina, St Julian's, Naxxar, Gozo and private villas across the island are all part of our Malta work.

How do we reserve our date?

Tell us about your celebration through the enquiry form. Once we confirm availability, a signed agreement and booking retainer secure your date.
