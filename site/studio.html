The Studio

We were behind the lights long before the lens

Effects Tech Studio · Malta

Effects Tech has spent years designing and producing some of India's most celebrated weddings and events. Our studio photographs and films them the way only a production house can: knowing every cue before it happens.

I

We know the cue sheet

When the lights drop for the first dance or the cannons fire over the harbour, we are already in position, because we know the running order as well as the producers do.

II

Quiet, invisible crews

Small, discreet teams who move with the celebration. Your guests will remember the evening, not the cameras.

III

Planned around the light

Malta's light moves fast. We build every wedding day around it, from first light in Mdina to blue hour over Valletta.
